# Donor Reference Capture Checklist

Record version/hash and capture method for every measurement.

## Player

- eye height;
- standing/crouching height;
- walk/run speed;
- acceleration/deceleration feel;
- interaction distance;
- pickup behavior;
- throw behavior.

## Garage and world

- garage dimensions;
- door sizes and pivots;
- workbench positions;
- nearby road width and elevation;
- key location coordinates/distances;
- shoreline and terrain elevations;
- day length and sunrise/sunset behavior.

## Vehicle

- shell dimensions;
- wheelbase/track width;
- mass;
- part masses;
- mount positions;
- bolt counts and tool sizes;
- steering lock;
- suspension travel;
- gear ratios;
- idle and redline;
- start/stall behavior;
- acceleration windows;
- braking distance;
- fluid capacities;
- electrical prerequisites.

## Audio

- idle/load transitions;
- interior/exterior balance;
- starter duration;
- transmission whine;
- gravel and body impacts;
- rain intensity layers;
- room/interior changes.

## Weather

- rain onset/decay;
- fog visibility;
- road wetness appearance;
- cloud transitions;
- wind/vegetation response.
