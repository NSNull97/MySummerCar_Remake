# Phase 1 domain checklist

Этот список — не авторитетный перечень контента, а набор категорий, которые
обязательно должны быть проверены в donor-аудите.

- Player movement, interaction and viewmodel actions
- Needs, sleep, stress, dirtiness, urine, eating, drinking, smoking, alcohol
- Home, sauna, utilities and domestic interactions
- Physical world items, tools, food, liquids, packaging and containers
- Satsuma assembly, tuning, wear, fluids, electrical and inspection state
- Every donor vehicle: player-drivable, service, AI-only and scripted
- Traffic, bus, train, boat and route/schedule behavior
- Every named and background NPC
- Dialogues, schedules, relationships and conditional behavior
- Stores, fuel, pub, Fleetari and other services
- Phone, mail, bills, catalog/order and deliveries
- Every job, repeatable activity and payment flow
- Story/event chains and donor progression outcomes
- Police, fines, checkpoints, jail, hospital, death/permadeath
- Rally registration, eligibility, course, timing, results and consequences
- Radio, TV, music import, computer, games, gambling and local events
- World triggers, doors, utilities, clocks, calendars and scene transitions
- Save state for every above domain
- Temporary legacy models, textures, materials, animations and audio feedback
- Known donor quirks, exploits and differences policy
